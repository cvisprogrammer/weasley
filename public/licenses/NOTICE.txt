This distribution bundles @mlc-ai/web-llm 0.2.86 (Apache-2.0) and model execution libraries published by mlc-ai/binary-mlc-llm-libs. The WebLLM npm bundle contains its compiled runtime, tokenizer, and grammar dependencies. Preserve upstream copyright and license notices. Model weights are downloaded separately from the model repositories listed in registry.json. Review their current licenses and the upstream binary-library notices before public redistribution; this prototype is not a completed legal/license review.

Version 0.4 also bundles htmlparser2 and its DOM/entity helper dependencies for inert email-to-text conversion. Their license texts are included alongside this notice. No email HTML is rendered by these parsers.
